About Nature

Nature is an important part of our life. It provides us with air, water, food and many other resources. Forests, rivers, mountains and oceans help maintain the balance of our planet.

Why Should We Protect Nature?

  • Nature provides clean air and fresh water.
  • Forests provide homes for animals and birds.
  • Plants help reduce air pollution.
  • Protecting nature helps future generations.
